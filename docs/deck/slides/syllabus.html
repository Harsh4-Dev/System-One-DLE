What this shows: one project uses all four units together, and its goal is the course objective itself: identify the appropriate deep architecture for each application, here per input.

Our project touches all four units. Unit one: the TextCNN expert, backpropagation, batch normalisation, gradient clipping, AdamW with a cosine schedule. Unit two: CNN classifiers and training a new head on a frozen pretrained network. Unit three is the core: RNN, LSTM and GRU experts, embeddings, attention, a transformer head and a BERT-family encoder. Unit four: the REINFORCE router, a policy-gradient method with reward correct minus lambda times cost. And the course objective, identifying the right architecture for an application, is what ADAR automates.
