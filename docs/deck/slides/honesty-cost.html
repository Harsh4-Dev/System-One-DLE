What this shows: ADAR has the lowest log-loss of every model, so its probabilities are the most honest; the cascade matches its accuracy (93.32% vs 93.28%) with 2.6× less compute.

Accuracy is not the whole story. On the left is log-loss, which punishes confident mistakes; lower is better. ADAR's is 0.197, 15 percent lower than the best baseline, Stacking (Laya + experts), at 0.232, and Laya zero-shot is at 0.237. On the right is cost, CPU time per input. Laya on its own takes 706 ms. Running Laya plus ADAR on everything takes 492 ms. The cascade escalates only the hard inputs and averages 189 ms, 2.6× cheaper, at 93.3% accuracy instead of 93.3%.
