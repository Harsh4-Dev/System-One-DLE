What this shows: the router learns per dataset how often the big model is worth its cost: 36% of news articles, 0.1% of tweets.

This is the cascade as a simulation. Every dot is an input. The experts answer first, and the router decides: taupe dots are answered right away, coral dots go to Laya plus ADAR. The split matches our AG News run, where 36% escalate. We trained two routers: a threshold on the predicted probability that the experts are right, with the cut-off chosen on validation data, and a REINFORCE policy from Unit IV with reward one for a correct answer minus lambda times the cost. On AG News the cascade is 2.6× cheaper than always running Laya plus ADAR, at 93.3% instead of 93.5%. On Emotion it calls Laya for 0.1% of inputs and is 41× cheaper. A perfect router would reach 94.9% on AG News, so there is still room to improve the router.
