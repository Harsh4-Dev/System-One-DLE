What this shows: on AG News, 64% of inputs never touch the 395M-parameter model; the rest get both views, combined by the head.

Here is the full system. Text comes in on the left. Four small networks from our syllabus, TextCNN, RNN, LSTM and GRU, answer first; together they take 17 ms on a CPU. A router looks only at those experts and asks: are they sure? On AG News, 64% of the time they are, and we answer immediately. The other 36% escalate: the frozen Laya encoder reads the text, and the ADAR head combines Laya's view with the experts' votes, shown by the dashed line, to give the final answer plus calibrated probabilities.
