Our idea

Let small models answer first. Call the big model only when they are unsure, and let a trained head decide whom to trust.

Instead of choosing one architecture by hand for the whole dataset, ADAR learns the choice for each input.

That leads to one idea. Let the small, cheap syllabus networks answer first. Only when they are unsure do we call the big System-One model. And instead of us choosing the architecture by hand, a small trained head decides, for each input, which model to trust and how much.
