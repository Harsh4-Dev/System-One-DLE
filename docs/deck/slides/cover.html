Our project is ADAR, Adaptive Deep Architecture Routing. On the right is the whole idea: small networks from our syllabus, the experts; a big open System-One model called Laya; and ADAR, a small trained head that decides, for every input, which of them to trust and whether the big model is needed at all. Every number in this deck comes from one full run of our notebook on Kaggle with the real Laya model, 63 minutes end to end.
