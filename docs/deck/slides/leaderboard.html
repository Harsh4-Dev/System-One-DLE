What this shows: our scorer reproduces the board’s accuracy (0.766 vs 0.766), so the calibration metrics we add are comparable to it.

We also tested on a public System-One benchmark, typed-decisions: 400 cases and 2,000 typed decisions. First, a sanity check: for Laya's fine-tuned checkpoint our scorer gives accuracy 0.766; the board lists 0.766. The board does not list its calibration, so we add it: Brier 0.061, KL 0.117 and ECE 0.214. Second, we applied ADAR's calibration step, per question-type temperatures fitted only on the train split. On base Laya, calibration error goes from 0.173 to 0.021 and KL from 0.569 to 0.433, with accuracy unchanged at 0.363. On the fine-tuned checkpoint the effect is mixed: ECE 0.214 to 0.063, Brier 0.061 to 0.148, partly because that checkpoint was trained on the same train split.
